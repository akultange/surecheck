[09] FAQs

Questions, answered.

Have a pregnancy test question in mind? Chances are someone else had the same one.

When can I use the test?

From the first day of a missed period — at any time of the day.

I have an irregular period. How do I know when to test?

Test 19 days after you think you may have conceived. If the result is negative, test again 48 hours later.

What if the line in the test region (T) is very pale?

A pale second line still means you are pregnant.

Can results be read after 10 minutes?

No. Results read after the 10th minute should be disregarded.

What can affect the result?

Certain medications can. Testing within 8 weeks after childbirth or an abortion may give a false positive because of residual hCG — if you're concerned, ask a doctor to interpret the result. Drinking a lot of liquid can lower the concentration of hCG. Birth control pills, antibiotics, painkillers and alcohol do not affect the result.

I tested “pregnant”, then “not pregnant” — or my period started. What does this mean?

The test is over 99% accurate from the first day of a missed period, and a positive result — even a pale one — means pregnancy. Early pregnancy loss is possible, so a later negative result or the start of a period can happen. If you're unsure, talk to your doctor.

A pink background or streaks appeared in the result window. Is this a concern?

No. Urine composition and room humidity can cause streaking or a pink background. This does not affect the result.
