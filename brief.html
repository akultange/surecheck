How it was checked

Measured on the live build. The numbers are repeatable: the tests are scripts, not screenshots picked by hand.

91

Lighthouse performance on a mid-range phone over simulated slow 4G (median of three runs). Largest paint 2.46 s, layout shift 0, best practices 100.

0

Accessibility violations in axe-core (WCAG 2.2 A/AA plus best practice), desktop and phone. Lighthouse accessibility 100.

0 px

Text showing inside the header, at every quarter screen of the page, arriving scrolling down and scrolling up: 124 states on desktop, 158 on phone. Its bar matches the colours beneath it to within 1%.

AA

Contrast on every text colour pair: ink on lilac 11.7:1, white on plum 9.3:1, white on crimson 6.3:1.

6

Languages the type already covers: EN, BG, RO, MK, SQ and AR (Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic), with fallbacks matched to each font so text never jumps as fonts load.

99.6%

Overlap between the 3D Streamer’s front view and your official render.
